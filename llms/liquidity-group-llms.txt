# LIQUiDITY Group

> LIQUiDITY Group (branded "Liquidity") is an AI-native private credit lender and alternative
> asset manager founded in 2018, headquartered in New York with offices in Tel Aviv, London,
> Singapore, Tokyo and Abu Dhabi. It deploys non-dilutive debt and growth capital — roughly
> $10M to $200M per facility — to late-stage technology and mid-market companies in 35+
> countries, underwritten by its proprietary DYNAMiCs decision-science platform. It manages
> several billion dollars across multiple debt and equity funds, including Mars Growth Capital,
> a joint venture with MUFG Bank, and is backed by MUFG, Apollo Asset Management, Spark Capital
> and Meitav Dash.

## The DYNAMiCs platform (private, credentials required)

The company's product runs at https://app.liquiditygroup.com — a single-page application
titled "Liquidity Capital - Rev-share Growth Financing", entered at
https://app.liquiditygroup.com/v2/signin. Its own publicly served JavaScript bundle names two
HTTP surfaces, https://app.liquiditygroup.com/graphql and https://app.liquiditygroup.com/api/v3/,
and its robots.txt disallows /cabinet/, /analyst/, /investors/ and /distributors/ — the four
private role areas of the platform. Both surfaces are authentication-gated: unauthenticated
GraphQL returns HTTP 400 {"errors":[{"message":"Something went wrong"}]} and the REST namespace
returns HTTP 404 with a JSON error envelope. No schema could be introspected without
credentials, and no schema is published anywhere. Nothing about those surfaces is documented
here beyond their existence, and none of it is a public integration surface.

Note that the SPA answers HTTP 200 with the same HTML shell for every path, including
/.well-known/agent-card.json, /openapi.json and /llms.txt. Those 200s are catch-all shells,
not documents. Do not treat them as evidence of a published artifact.
